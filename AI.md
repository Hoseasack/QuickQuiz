# AI - Usage and Intentions

This document lays out the places where AI has/was used, what my intentions are, future goals, and my stance on AI.

### What parts contain/use AI?

AI was used to generate the foundation of QuickQuiz. Such as the initial files/commits for the server, HTML, CSS, JS, etc.. I have a limited coding experience, and as such, used AI to build a strong starting point.

Keeping this is mind, **AI will not be used for _any_ future development past the initial commits.**

### Which AI was used?

The AI used to generate, diagnose, or otherwise contribute with was Claude Code.

### Why not continue to use AI?

Even though the use of AI has made QuickQuiz possible, it should not be a continued requierment to sustain development. In addition, QuickQuiz is a personal project with the sole goals of:
- Being a useful tool that benifits at least one person.
- Allowing me the space/enviornment to learn and practice my skills.
  - As well as allowing others to do the same.

### What if I want to use AI?

Like I said before, QuickQuiz is a space for people to practice and learn _without_ AI. If this doesn't align with your beleifs, feel free to fork QuickQuiz and vibe code away. Otherwise, I ask that you avoid using AI for generating fixes to issues, new features, pull requests, etc..

### Why are you doing this to yourself? AI is the future, so learn to use it to increase efficiency!

While AI has become integrated into every aspect of life, including coding, AI is not you or your brain. Whatever happened to problem-solving or critical thinking? Letting AI solve the problems it created based on the ideas it generated using the plan it developed seems dystopian. The human aspect to collaboration and creation is what makes this rewarding. Also, there is a distinct difference between vibe-coding and using AI to assist development, such as with debugging, cleaning up unused fragments, and automating deployment. **I am explicitly talking about vibe-coding.**
